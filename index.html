Overview

PI & JU Anglo-Saxon International College is a strictly Anglo-Saxon private secondary institution equipped with full boarding facilities, committed to academic excellence, moral integrity, and leadership development. Located in Mbiteng, Yaoundé (Cameroon), the college provides an authentic, high-standard English-educational environment.

Pure Anglo-Saxon Curriculum

Our academic framework is 100% modeled on the traditional Anglo-Saxon education system:

  • Academic Cycles: Lower Secondary (Form 1 to Form 5) & High School (Lower Sixth and Upper Sixth).
  • Official Examinations: Comprehensive preparation for the GCE Ordinary Level (O-Level) and GCE Advanced Level (A-Level) examinations.
  • Language of Instruction: Strictly English across all subjects, school operations, and activities.

Boarding Facilities

Our boarding school offers safe, comfortable, and separate residential halls for boys and girls:

  • 24/7 Supervision: Dedicated housemasters, housemistresses, and academic tutors providing continuous care and guidance.
  • Supervised Prep Sessions: Daily compulsory evening study hours to guarantee consistent academic performance.
  • Balanced Dining: Nutritious meals served daily in a hygienic environment.
  • Secure Campus: Fully fenced, monitored campus with basic medical assistance on site.
