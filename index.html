Welcome to Our Institution

Our college offers a stimulating and nurturing learning environment that fosters the academic and personal growth of every student. Through educational excellence and a wide array of learning activities, we prepare tomorrow’s citizens to successfully meet every challenge.

Overview

PI & JU Anglo-Saxon International College is a strictly Anglo-Saxon private secondary institution equipped with full boarding facilities, committed to academic excellence, moral integrity, and leadership development. Located in Mbiteng, Yaoundé (Cameroon), the college provides an authentic, high-standard English-educational environment.

Education

Anglo-Saxon Curriculum & Courses Dispensed

Our college delivers a pure Anglo-Saxon academic curriculum designed to prepare students for official GCE Ordinary Level (O-Level) and Advanced Level (A-Level) examinations.

Lower Secondary (Form 1 to Form 5)

Comprehensive foundation courses across general subjects:

  • Languages: English Language.
  • Sciences & ICT: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science.
  • Humanities & Commercials: History, Geography, Economics, Citizenship Education, Commerce, Principles of Accounts.

High School (Lower Sixth & Upper Sixth)

Specialized fields tailored for university entrance and academic excellence:

  • Science Stream: Pure Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science.
  • Arts Stream: Literature in English, History, Geography, Economics, Philosophy.
