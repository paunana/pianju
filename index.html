Welcome to Our Institution

Our college offers a stimulating and nurturing learning environment that fosters the academic and personal growth of every student. Through educational excellence and a wide array of learning activities, we prepare tomorrow’s citizens to successfully meet every challenge.

Overview

PI & JU Anglo-Saxon International College is a strictly Anglo-Saxon private secondary institution equipped with full boarding facilities, committed to academic excellence, moral integrity, and leadership development. Located in Mbiteng, Yaoundé (Cameroon), the college provides an authentic, high-standard English-educational environment.

Pure Anglo-Saxon Curriculum

Our academic framework is 100% modeled on the traditional Anglo-Saxon education system:

  • Academic Cycles: Lower Secondary (Form 1 to Form 5) & High School (Lower Sixth and Upper Sixth).
  • Official Examinations: Comprehensive preparation for the GCE Ordinary Level (O-Level) and GCE Advanced Level (A-Level) examinations.
  • Language of Instruction: Strictly English across all subjects, school operations, and daily activities.

Boarding Facilities

Our boarding facilities offer a safe, structured, and comfortable home-away-from-home environment with separate residential halls for boys and girls:

  • 24/7 Pastoral Supervision: Dedicated housemasters, housemistresses, and resident academic tutors ensuring student welfare and discipline.
  • Supervised Evening Prep: Mandatory daily study hours designed to cultivate academic diligence and consistent performance.
  • Nutritious Dining: Balanced, healthy meals prepared in a highly hygienic environment.
  • Secure Campus Environment: A fully fenced, guarded campus with continuous monitoring and basic medical care facilities on site.
